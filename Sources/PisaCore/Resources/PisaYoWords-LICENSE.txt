PisaYoWords contains lowercase word forms with ё, excluding spellings with
competing е/ё forms, other held-out spellings and entries without a common-word
analysis. The list is sorted, deduplicated and compressed.
PisaEntityForms contains geographical names and singular given names, surnames
and patronymics, excluding forms also marked as organizations or trademarks.
The list is folded from ё to е, sorted, deduplicated and compressed.
PisaCommonForms contains ordinary Russian word forms up to 48 characters.
The list excludes organization and trademark spellings and is folded from ё
to е, sorted, deduplicated and compressed.

To reproduce the lists, run the following command.
python3 scripts/generate-yo-wordforms.py \
  --entity-output Sources/PisaCore/Resources/PisaEntityForms.deflate \
  --common-output Sources/PisaCore/Resources/PisaCommonForms.deflate
